Part I

The World

Romulon

The golden domes

The known world turns on its golden domes, seat of the high elves and heart of the Church. Its faith claims to hold up the very structure of reality, and few who kneel there ask what lies beneath it.

Forgelight

The snowbound north

Far from that light, in the snowbound north, a hamlet clings to the ruins of an ancient snow-elven temple, digging into a past that was buried for a reason.

Black Iron

The king's roads

And on the king's roads between them, a host in black iron marches, burning everything in its path.

A priest with a secret mission, a rogue with a voice inside her head, and a stranger with a blade drawn from his own blood are about to meet in a tavern at the edge of the first snow. What they are looking for, and what is looking for them, is for the table to find out.

Part II

What's Inside

Apotheosis is a dark fantasy campaign built on fifth edition, with its own lore, peoples, and rules that lean into consequence and story.

Homebrew Rules
Tiered travel, class divergence, critical roll effects, group rolls, and the Act of Valour and Heroic Death options for when a character refuses to fall.
Gestalt Characters
A high-powered variant where small parties advance in two classes at once.
Species
Reimagined elves, dwerfen, orqs, golyaths, drakeborn, seraphs, tieflings and more, plus half-blood and variant species such as the Dhampir, Chymaera, and Reborn.
Religions
Rival faiths tied to the pillars of reality, each with its own domain, clergy, and history of holy war.
Afflictions
Diseases, curses and transformations, from lyrium and red rot to lycanthropy and vampirism.
Magic
Four sources of power: synthetic, occult, divine, and natural.
Bestiary
Original monsters and a playable monster class.
Part III

Playing

The campaign uses a hybrid of the 2014 and 2024 Player's Handbooks. Players should read the homebrew rules and species chapters before building a character; the rest of the world reveals itself in play.

Alpha Rules and lore change between sessions, and some chapters are still being written.
