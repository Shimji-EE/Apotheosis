Part I

The World

Apotheosis is a land of frozen mud and woodsmoke, of gallows creaking at the crossroads and crows grown fat on what hangs there. Children cough through winters that take the old and the weak, tithe-men carry off the last sack of grain, and the bells ring for the dead more often than for prayer. The faithful still kneel on cold stone before an old church that sells salvation by the coin, but beneath its incense and gold leaf, something has long since begun to rot. And everyone, from the beggar picking lice in the gutter to the king on his gilded chair, dreams of rising above the filth.

Where that rot spreads, monsters follow. Wolves that walk like men drag shepherds from their fires, a miller's wife claws her way back out of the grave still hungry, and the neighbour who prayed too hard for more now prowls the churchyard on all fours. Under flooded crypts and barrow hills lie crowns, reliquaries and saints' bones wrapped in gold, each one whispering of a way up, and every hand that pries them loose pays in blood, silver, or something it cannot name.

Older than any crown or creed, other hungers stir. Fishermen haul up nets heavy with eyes, and whole villages wake to black water in the wells and children muttering in tongues no priest can read. These things dwell past the edge of the maps and the reach of prayer, and they turn the wheels of this world as surely as the seasons, feeding on every mortal who tries to climb.

For that is the oldest sin of all: apotheosis, the dream of ascending to sit upon the throne of heaven. Kings have bled whole provinces dry for it, saints have starved themselves to bone, and beasts have devoured their own kind, and the land is littered with what remained of them: husks rotting in iron gibbets, cathedrals burned to their footings, names scratched from every record. That throne was never made for mortal or beast. It belongs to the One who lit the stars, and what becomes of those who forget it is for the table to find out.

Part II

Playing

The campaign uses a hybrid of the 2014 and 2024 Player's Handbooks. Players should read the homebrew rules and species chapters before building a character; the rest of the world reveals itself in play.

Alpha Rules and lore change between sessions, and some chapters are still being written.
